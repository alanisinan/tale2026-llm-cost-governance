===== INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY 7.20 (EXCERPT) - Section 5: Incident Reports =====
5.1 A written incident report must be produced for every incident rated Medium or higher within
three (3) business days of the incident being declared. The report is owned by the incident handler
and approved by the SOC manager.
5.2 The report must be factual and must distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions. Times must be
recorded in UTC. Personal data must be minimized: refer to users by account name rather than full
name where possible.
5.3 Reports involving Restricted data (for example financial account numbers, tax identifiers, or
health information) must be shared with the Office of General Counsel and the Privacy Office, who
determine whether regulatory or contractual breach-notification obligations apply.
5.4 Reports are stored in the IR case management system and retained for seven (7) years.
5.5 Lessons-learned items must be tracked as action items with an owner and due date.
5.6 Templates are available on the SOC SharePoint site. Use of the template is recommended but not
mandatory.
